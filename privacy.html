Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Muhammad Nadeem ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses and protects information when you use our social media scheduling service at nodiz.posti.win (the "Service"). The Service lets you connect social media accounts, including TikTok, and schedule or upload content to them.

1. Information we collect

When you connect a TikTok account, we receive the following from TikTok, only with your permission:

  • Basic profile information: your TikTok display name, profile picture and a unique account identifier.
  • Profile details: such as your profile link, bio and verification status.
  • Access tokens that allow the Service to upload and publish content to your account on your behalf.

We also store:

  • Content you create or upload to the Service, such as videos, images, captions, titles and scheduled times.
  • Account information you provide when you sign up, such as your name and email address.
  • Technical information needed to run the Service, such as server logs (IP address, browser type, timestamps) and essential cookies that keep you signed in.

We never receive your TikTok password, and we do not access your private messages or contacts.

2. How we use your information

  • To upload and publish the content you schedule to the TikTok account you connected, including sending drafts to your TikTok inbox for you to review and post.
  • To show you which account your content will be posted to.
  • To operate, maintain, secure and troubleshoot the Service.
  • To contact you about your account or the Service.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your TikTok data for advertising.

3. How we share information

We share information only as needed to provide the Service:

  • With TikTok, to upload and publish your content through TikTok's official APIs. TikTok's handling of your data is governed by the TikTok Privacy Policy.
  • With service providers that host our servers or store media files, who process data only on our instructions.
  • When required by law, or to protect the rights, safety and security of our users or the Service.

4. Data retention

We keep your TikTok access tokens and profile information only while your TikTok account is connected to the Service. When you disconnect it, we delete the tokens. Content you upload is kept until you delete it or close your account, after which it is removed from our systems within 30 days, unless we are required by law to keep it.

5. Your choices and rights

  • Disconnect TikTok at any time from the Service's channel settings, or revoke our access in the TikTok app's settings, where you manage apps connected to your account.
  • Access, correct or delete your information by emailing us at ayman.nadeem3@outlook.com. We will respond within 30 days.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local privacy laws. Contact us to exercise them.

6. Where we store data and how we protect it

Your information is stored on servers we control or rent, which may be located outside your country. We use reasonable safeguards to protect it, including encrypted HTTPS connections and restricted access to our servers. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Children

The Service is not directed to children under 13, and you must meet TikTok's minimum age requirements to connect a TikTok account. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the "Last updated" date above. Continuing to use the Service after an update means you accept the revised policy.

9. Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, contact Muhammad Nadeem at ayman.nadeem3@outlook.com.
